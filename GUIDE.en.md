# Guide

> **Important for error reports:** switch on the **Diagnostic log** at the bottom of the page *before* you connect to the scooter. Only then is the full connection handshake captured - and those are exactly the lines we need in a [ticket](https://github.com/Laufbursche42/Laufbursche42/issues) to reproduce a problem.

## What you need
- An RCB e-scooter (Tuya/Smart Life app `com.rcb.ytd`).
- A phone or computer with **Chrome**, **Edge**, or on iOS **Bluefy**. Safari and Firefox cannot do Web Bluetooth.
- To write, also your Tuya localKey and an srand from the pairing (see below).

## Connecting
1. Turn on Bluetooth and wake the scooter.
2. Tap **Connect** and pick the scooter from the list.
3. If it is not listed, tick **Show all devices** and try again. The real check is the Tuya GATT profile found, not the advertised name.
4. Once connected, the live-values and settings cards appear.

## Keys and session
The Tuya DP channel is encrypted. The session key is `secretKey5 = MD5(localKey + srand)`.
- You get the **localKey** from your Tuya account (for example via the Tuya IoT platform). It is not in the Bluetooth traffic and is never generated by this page.
- **srand** is 6 bytes from the scooter pairing reply.
- Fill in both fields and the page shows the derived key. The derivation runs fully locally.
- Optional: upload a Bluetooth capture. The page searches it locally and shows srand and dpId candidates as suggestions. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is proven.

## Device schema
The numeric dpIds are defined per product in the Tuya cloud and are not in the app package. In the schema, map each code to its dpId as JSON, for example `{"headlight_switch":104,"boost":{"dpId":110,"type":"bool"}}`. Only with a dpId and the session key can a row write.

## Reading live values
The scooter reports data points over the DP channel. A tile shows a value once a reported dpId matches a code from your schema. A dash just means that value has not come in yet. Without the session key, encrypted frames stay unreadable.

## Writing settings
The **Settings** card holds the Tuya control codes the app package carries (headlight, LED, cruise, zero-start, unit, auto-unlock, move-alarm, find and boost). Each row needs its dpId from the schema and a valid session key. Risky writes such as boost or auto-unlock ask first. Important: an echo in the log only means the scooter accepted the frame. Only a changed live value proves the effect.

## Advanced settings (engine level)
Here you build a DP frame by hand from dpId, type, value and protocol version. Preview only builds and logs it; live sends it encrypted.

## Shortcuts
Copy the link to your home screen, then one tap opens the page and tries to connect. On iOS via Bluefy, and the scooter must have been connected normally once before.

## If something does not work
- Cannot connect? Check that the browser supports Web Bluetooth, Bluetooth is on and the scooter is awake. Retry with **Show all devices**.
- Row stays disabled? Then the dpId is missing from the schema, or the session key (localKey and srand) is not set.
- Nothing happens after a command? Check the log: if it says "sent", the frame was written, but only a changed live value proves the effect.
- **Diagnostics: list all devices** in the log area shows every Bluetooth service of a device without writing anything - useful for support.

## Contribute
Want to find out if and how tuning works on your scooter? Test this tool on your own vehicle and open a ticket on [GitHub](https://github.com/Laufbursche42/Laufbursche42/issues) - with your model and what worked (or did not). That way we figure out together what is possible on which model.
